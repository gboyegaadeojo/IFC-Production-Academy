# Decision: a first pilot of Video Directing Concepts (2026-11-03)

**Status:** decided by G, 2026-11-03. Changes decision 3 in [2026-10-31 open decisions](2026-10-31-open-decisions.md). The start date is a default that G can change.

## Why

The videos are coming sooner than planned: G expects them all by about 13 Nov 2026. So the first pilot can run about eight weeks ahead of the 10 Jan 2027 plan.

## What changes

| | Earlier plan | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Course** | Camera Operations Level 1 | Video Directing Concepts (Camera Directing, Foundations) |
| **Start** | Sun 10 Jan 2027 | Tue 17 Nov 2026 |
| **People** | 4 to 6 volunteers, at least two new and two experienced | G's two accounts: one admin and observer, one plain volunteer |

The volunteer account had its admin roles removed for the pilot, so it shows what a volunteer really sees and can do. The roles can be restored afterwards.

G will add the course's skills and quiz later. Until then the pilot covers lessons and progress only. Quiz scoring, the practical, the competency and Approved to Serve stay untested until they're added.

## What this doesn't settle

Two accounts held by one person can't show whether volunteers find the app easy to use, or give the completion figures SPEC §9 asks for. So this first pilot doesn't meet the Phase 4 exit criteria or replace the go or no-go review. G hasn't yet decided:

- when real volunteers try it, and on which course
- whether Camera Operations Level 1 and its November curriculum check and filming dates still stand
- whether the 21 Feb 2027 ministry-wide launch date moves

Real volunteers still aren't invited or emailed until G says so.

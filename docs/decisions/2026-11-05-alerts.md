# Decision: notifications, reminders, and phone alerts (2026-11-05)

**Status:** decided by G, 2026-11-05. Nothing here sends to real volunteers before launch: the switch in [the notifications runbook](../runbooks/notifications.md) still holds email and alerts.

## Ask

G wants notifications for the events that matter, a view on whether alerts beat many emails, reminders taken into account, a way to see who hasn't turned on alerts, and alerts to be a must.

## Decided

- **Serving reminders come from both the Academy and Planning Center.** G chose this knowing some people will get both. It replaces "Planning Center sends its own, so the Academy doesn't".
- **Reminders:** please confirm after 48 hours, two days before serving, and that morning; an assigned course not started after 7 days; the existing course nudges at 7 and 21 days. Learning reminders are limited to one a day per person, and serving reminders aren't limited.
- **Quiet hours cover phones too,** 20:00 to 08:00 Winnipeg time. This replaces "quiet hours apply to email only" in [the app and alerts decision](2026-11-01-app-and-alerts.md).
- **New events:** a service moved or cancelled, a join request (to leaders), a spot still needing cover close to the service (to schedulers), Approved to Serve, a certificate ready, a new course, an SOP updated, and a shared mentoring note. Mentors' private notes never notify anyone, and a shared note's words never go in the alert.
- **Which way each travels (G chose these from the options offered):** low-priority news (new course, certificate, SOP update) stays in the app. Everything else is a phone alert, each time rather than a daily summary. Someone with alerts on a phone gets no routine email. A moved or cancelled service comes by alert and email. Each person chooses by kind what reaches their phone. Email is the backup, partly because Resend's free plan allows 100 emails a day.
- **Emails use the "Calm" design** (G chose it from four mocks: Calm, Bold, Bright, and Academy). They're centred on a soft grey page, with the app's icon, a large title that's also the subject, the message as the inbox preview, and one rounded button that says what it does. Built with tables and inline styles, and no SVG, because email programs drop it. They show the app's current logo, and the details box and shorter phone alerts from the mock. Wording otherwise stays as it was. Sign-in emails match: the app sends them through Supabase's Send Email hook and Resend, so the design lives in one place.
- **Alerts are a required step, not a lock.** No website can turn alerts on for someone: each person has to allow them, and an iPhone needs the Home Screen app and iOS 16.4 or later. So people on a team are asked on each visit until it's done, with "Not now", and "My phone can't do this" for phones that can't. Nothing is locked, because locking would shut out older phones and go against training being open to everyone.
- **Who sees the list:** Department Leaders for their own teams, and Production Directors and Platform Admins for everyone (capability `alerts.view`). It shows on **Admin → Phone alerts**.
- **A phone is what counts.** Alerts on a computer alone show as "Computer only", and the person is still asked.

## Limit

Someone can turn alerts off in their phone's settings without the app knowing until a send fails, so "On" means the last we heard.

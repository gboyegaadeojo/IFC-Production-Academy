# Decision: notifications, reminders, and phone alerts (2026-11-05)

**Status:** decided by G, 2026-11-05. Nothing here sends to real volunteers before launch: the switch in [the notifications runbook](../runbooks/notifications.md) still holds email and alerts.

## Ask

G wants notifications for the events that matter, a view on whether alerts beat many emails, reminders taken into account, a way to see who hasn't turned on alerts, and alerts to be a must.

## Decided

- **Serving reminders come from both the Academy and Planning Center.** G chose this knowing some people will get both. It replaces "Planning Center sends its own, so the Academy doesn't".
- **Alerts are a required step, not a lock.** No website can turn alerts on for someone: each person has to allow them, and an iPhone needs the Home Screen app and iOS 16.4 or later. So people on a team are asked on each visit until it's done, with "Not now", and "My phone can't do this" for phones that can't. Nothing is locked, because locking would shut out older phones and go against training being open to everyone.
- **Who sees the list:** Department Leaders for their own teams, and Production Directors and Platform Admins for everyone (capability `alerts.view`). It shows on **Admin → Phone alerts**.
- **A phone is what counts.** Alerts on a computer alone show as "Computer only", and the person is still asked.

## Limit

Someone can turn alerts off in their phone's settings without the app knowing until a send fails, so "On" means the last we heard.

# Sign-in: option A, "Focus"

**Date:** 2026-11-06. **Decided by:** G, after clicking through three sign-in mocks (A Focus, B
Stage, C Preview) on a phone and the web.

## What G chose

Option A replaces the photo layout from 2026-11-01:

- A plain page in the app's theme, with light or dark at the top right.
- A row of department colours, then a large headline, "Learn it. Serve it.", and one line on what
  the Academy is for.
- **Continue with Planning Center** as the one main button. **Sign in with an email link** is a
  second, quieter button that opens the email form in its place. It starts open after an error.
- Centred on wide screens. On a phone the buttons sit low, within reach of a thumb.

The stock photo is no longer used and has been removed (`docs/assets.md`).

## What stays

How sign-in works doesn't change: Planning Center governs sign-in for the production team, and the
email link stays for anyone else (`docs/runbooks/planning-center-sign-in.md`).

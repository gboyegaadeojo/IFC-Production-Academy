# Side bar B and phone bar A

**Date:** 2026-11-09
**Decided by:** G, from the clickable mocks (three side bar options, three phone bar options).

## Side bar (768px and up)

Option B, a labelled side bar, replaces mock 5's 96px icon rail, which ran out of room: at a laptop's height the last link was cut off and the rail scrolled.

- 176px wide, with each link's name beside its icon. A button at the top folds it to 68px, icons only; the device remembers the choice (`academy_sidebar` cookie, so it loads the right width).
- Training and Messages first, then **Learn** (Courses, Explore) and **Serve** (Schedule, Checklist, SOPs, Service Dashboard) under small headings. Every page keeps its own link.
- Small type, chosen by G in two steps: links 9px, headings and your name 8px.
- Your name and "Settings, sign out" at the bottom open the account menu, which now holds the light or dark switch. The bell beside it shows the unread count.

## Phone bar (below 768px)

Option A, the sliding pill.

- The bar floats 12px in from each side, with a gap beneath it: 12px above the screen's edge, or just inside an iPhone's home bar area.
- A soft purple pill springs across to the tab you tap, and that tab's icon lifts a little. Pages reached from the tabs fade up into place.
- Lessons, chats, and a department's Continue bar don't animate, so their own fixed bars never move. Reduced motion turns all of it off.
- The tabs themselves are unchanged (decisions of 2026-11-04 and 2026-11-08).

## Notifications on phones (2026-11-10)

G chose option B of three mocks: the panel pulls down from under the top bar instead of rising from the bottom, as a card 8px in from the sides with a point at the bell (from option A), and the bell tips a little as it opens (from option C). Unread notifications are listed under **New** and the rest under **Earlier**, and filter chips (All, Serving, Training, Leading) appear when there's more than one kind. The grouping and filters apply to the side bar's panel too. A handle at the foot closes it.

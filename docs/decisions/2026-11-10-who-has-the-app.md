# Who has the app

**Date:** 2026-11-10
**Decided by:** G, from a clickable mock (computer and phone).

- Leaders see who on their teams has the Academy installed, beside whether their alerts are on, on **Admin → Phone alerts and app** (renamed from Phone alerts). Numbers at the top; filters for No app, Alerts not on, Some alerts off and Phone can't; on a phone, one short row each with **App** and **Alerts** marks. No last-opened or last-alert dates (G).
- **Some off** shows when alerts are on but someone has switched some kinds off for their phone, as "Off for: Training".
- The list stays in Admin, where only leaders can open it. Leaders also get a **Your teams** card at the foot of their own Notification settings that leads there, so it's found where they think about alerts.
- The app notes, once a day at most, when it's opened from a Home Screen, with only the kind of device (`record_app_open`, `app_installs`). A phone doesn't report a deleted app, so this means "has opened the installed app".

# Admin: assessments and competencies

## How it fits together

- A **competency** is a skill or piece of equipment someone has shown they can handle.
- A **serving role** lists the competencies it needs.
- A **practical assessment** is how someone earns a competency.
- **Approved to Serve** follows automatically once someone holds all of a role's competencies.

The observer records what they saw. The system decides the result.

## Assessments

**Waiting for assessment** lists volunteers who asked to be assessed. Choose **Assess now**. To record one nobody requested, use **Record an assessment**, and choose the practical and the person you watched. The list shows the department's members and anyone else who has started training there.

1. Watch them work.
2. Tick what you saw.
3. Add feedback. It's worth writing, especially for a "Not yet".

The Academy works out the result from the leader's pass rule. You don't pick the outcome. A "Not yet" has no waiting period: they can ask again when they're ready.

**Recent assessments** keeps each one with who observed it, what they saw, and the result. If one was recorded by mistake, **Withdraw this assessment**, with a reason. It stays on record, marked as withdrawn.

## Competencies and roles

**Competencies:** add the skills and equipment people need to show they can handle. Choose **Edit or archive** to change one. Each can have an expiry, if a skill needs refreshing.

**Serving roles:** add the roles people serve in, such as "Camera 2 operator", then choose what each one needs.

**People:** competencies appear here when someone passes a practical. If there's a reason to stop relying on one, choose **Suspend**, with a reason. You can restore it later.

## Serving teams

Teams are covered in [Scheduling](admin-scheduling.md). Passing an assessment never adds anyone to a team, and "Trained" or "Still learning" shows beside each name but doesn't gate anything.

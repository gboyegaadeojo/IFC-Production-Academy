# Admin: checklists

These pages set up what each position does before and after a service. For how the team uses them, see [Checklist](checklist.md).

## Task lists

A **task list** is a full set of department checklists. New services use the **Default** list, and keep the list they started with. For a special service, make another list and choose it for that service. Make one as **An empty list**, or **A copy of** another.

Hiding a list keeps it under **Hidden lists**. You can't hide the default.

## Making a checklist

1. **Add a checklist** to a department. One per kind of position works well, such as Camera Operators.
2. **Paste a list:** one item on each line. Start a line with `#` to begin a section. Bullets and numbers are removed for you. You can also add sections and items one at a time.
3. **Who it's for:** choose the positions. People scheduled in these positions see this checklist first. Everyone serving can still see the rest.

## Changing one

Use **Edit, move or remove** on any section or item. If you remove a task, it disappears from current and future checklists. Past services keep their records, and you can bring it back from **Hidden items**.

## Hidden items

Anything you remove shows up here, newest first, with **Restore**. If what something belongs to is hidden too, restore that first.

## Activity log

**Activity log** shows every tick, untick, reset, and undo at a service, including attempts that were refused, and every change to the checklists. It can't be edited or deleted. Use it to work out what happened.

## Service history

**Service history** shows what was done and what was missed at each service. A service's record is fixed when its day ends, so later checklist changes never alter it.

## Checklist settings

For church-wide checklist managers:

- **Service type:** the checklist opens to the next service of this type. Other services still have checklists.
- **Service day:** when nothing is published yet, the checklist still opens for this day so the team can tick. When Planning Center publishes the service, everything ticked stays with it.

## If someone can't see their checklist

Usually their Planning Center position isn't linked. The Task lists page warns you about it. Link it on the [Planning Center page](admin-scheduling.md).

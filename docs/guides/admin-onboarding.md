# Admin: onboarding

Onboarding takes someone from "I want to serve" to ready for a team. You build each pathway's stages and steps. People move along by themselves, and you're told when they need you.

## How people get in

- **The sign-up page:** the public **Serve with us** page. Under **People in pathways**, choose **Share the sign-up page** to print it for the lobby or show it at a volunteer night, with a QR code.
- **You add them:** **Add someone**, for someone you met in the lobby. They start at the pathway's first step.
- **Joining a department** can start that department's pathway.

## Building a pathway

Set up **Join the team** first: everyone who wants to serve starts there, then moves on to a department's pathway.

Open **Onboarding pathways**. Add stages, such as "Week 1". Then drag steps from the **Step library** onto a stage, or press **Add**. Select a step to change what it does. A step marked **Needs setup** isn't finished, and **Waits here** means people pause on it until it's done.

The steps you can use:

| Step | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Send an email** | Start from a template, then edit the subject and message. `{first name}` becomes their first name. **Emails are held until volunteer emails are switched on at launch.** |
| **Notify leaders** | Tells the leaders who look after the pathway |
| **Follow-up task** | Something a leader has to do, with who reaches out and how many hours they have |
| **Pair a mentor** | Sets up [mentoring](mentoring.md) |
| **Assign a course** | Moves on by itself when they finish it |
| **Wait some days** | Pauses before the next step |
| **Choose a department** | The volunteer picks one on Training, under **Your next steps**. Leave departments unticked to offer every open one, including ones opened later |
| **Add to a team in Planning Center** | A task for a leader, which completes itself when the sync sees them on the team. Planning Center stays read-only |
| **Finish** | Ends the pathway |

To remove a stage, move or remove its steps first.

## Follow-ups

**Follow-ups** is what onboarding needs from you: **Yours**, **Anyone can take**, and **With other leaders**. Choose **I'll take this**, **Hand over** to someone else, or **Mark done**. Ticking one moves that person on to their next step.

## People in pathways

**People in pathways** shows who's where, how long they've been there, and what happens next. Open someone for their history and notes. A note can be shared with leaders who look after the pathway, or private to you.

If someone changes their mind, choose **Stop onboarding**. Their history and notes are kept, and you can add them again later.

## What it doesn't do

A pathway never adds anyone to a team and never approves anyone to serve. Courses only move a step on from real progress.

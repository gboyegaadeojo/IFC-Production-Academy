# Admin: people and departments

## Membership requests

**Membership requests** lists people asking to join your department, oldest first. Choose **Approve**. To add a note or decline, choose **More**. A note or a reason for declining is optional, and it's kept in the history.

Approving makes someone part of the department's team. It doesn't approve them to serve. Courses are already open to anyone.

The **Members** view lists who's in each department. To take someone out, choose **Remove**. Their progress and history are kept.

## Invitations

Use **Invitations** to bring someone straight into a department. They join when they accept, because you've already decided.

1. Choose **New invitation**.
2. Pick a department, or **The Academy only, no department**.
3. Say how many people can use it and how long the link works. A note for you is optional. Leave out personal details.
4. Copy the link, or show its QR code on a screen or print it. **It's shown only once,** for security.

To stop a link, choose **Revoke**. It stops working straight away, and people who already joined stay members.

## People and roles

**People and roles** lists everyone who has signed in, 50 to a page. Open a person to:

- **Grant a role,** choosing a department if the role needs one. Pick a role to see what it lets someone do.
- **Remove a role,** with a reason. Past roles stay listed.
- **Change their account:** Suspend, Deactivate, or Reactivate. Training history is kept whatever the status. On the People list, only accounts that aren't active have a label beside the name.

You can't change your own account status or remove your own roles, so one person can't lock everyone out by accident. Every change goes in the audit log.

The Service Dashboard Editor role is limited to 3 people, here as well as on the [dashboard's setup page](service-dashboard.md).

## Departments

**Departments** is for adding, opening, pausing, or renaming a department. Changes take effect straight away and go in the audit log. Nobody's training history changes.

A new department gets the three levels straight away. For each one, you choose:

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Open now. Coming soon, which is seen but can't be joined. Hidden while you set it up |
| **How people join** | They ask and a leader approves, anyone can join with no approval, or by invitation only. Joining never approves someone to serve. If you remove someone from a department anyone can join, they have to ask to come back |

Lighting Operations goes live just by changing its status here. No new release is needed.

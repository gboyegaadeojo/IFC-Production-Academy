# Admin: reports, mentoring, and the audit log

## Reports

**Reports** covers departments where you can view training reports. Choose a department and what to show:

- **Course completion by volunteer:** who's where in each course, with certificates.
- **Competencies and Approved to Serve by volunteer:** what each person holds.
- **Approved to Serve by department and role:** how many people are ready for each role. Aim for at least two approved people in every role, so one absence doesn't leave a gap.

- **Course progress:** pick a course and see how many people have finished, are in progress, or haven't started. Choose a number to filter the list. Choose a person to see the lessons they've marked complete, their best quiz score and how many tries it took, and their practical. This tab also covers General and Leadership.

A finished course is separate from being approved to serve, and the app never says a video was watched, only which lessons someone marked complete.

Reports include the department's members and anyone else who has started training there, because anyone can take any course. Starting a course doesn't make someone a member or put them on a team.

**Download CSV** gives you the same data as a file. Downloads go in the audit log. They include names and training records only, and never mentoring's private notes.

## Mentoring

**Mentoring** pairs a volunteer with a trainer or leader. Choose the mentor and the volunteer, then **Pair them**. Pairing needs at least one trainer or leader and one member in the department.

They share goals and notes. You can read the shared ones. **A mentor's private notes stay private, even from you.**

To finish, choose **End this pairing**, with an optional reason. It stays on record for both of them. See [Mentoring](mentoring.md) for what they see.

## Audit log

**Audit log** lists every sensitive change, newest first: who did it, what happened, and when, in Winnipeg time. Entries can't be edited or deleted.

Use it to answer "who changed this, and when?" It includes things like roles, memberships, publishing, overrides, report downloads, and Service Dashboard layouts and screens.

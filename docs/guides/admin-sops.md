# Admin: SOP library

**SOP library** holds the procedures for the departments you look after, and the ones for all of production.

## Finding an SOP

SOPs are grouped by category. Select a category's heading to collapse or open it, or use the category buttons to show one. You can also search, and filter by who it's for and its status. If any SOPs are due for review, a note at the top can show just those.

## Adding an SOP

1. Choose **Add SOP**. Give it a title, say who it's for, and choose a category or create a new one.
2. Write the steps in the page, attach a file, or do both. A file can be a PDF, PNG, or JPEG up to 10 MB, and it's stored privately.
3. Add a summary saying what it's for and when to use it. **Save draft** keeps your work.
4. Choose **Publish** and say what changed. The draft becomes the current version.

An SOP needs written steps or a file before it can be published.

## Writing the steps

Type in the page, and use the buttons above it to add headings, numbered steps, bullets, bold, and warnings. **Use a template** gives you a starting shape, and **Preview** shows what volunteers will see.

## SOPs for all of production

Choose **All of production** for a procedure that isn't about one department, such as how to do a copyright review. Everyone signed in can read the current version.

Only someone with organisation-wide rights, such as the Production Director or a Platform Administrator, can add or change these. Department leaders can attach them to their lessons.

## Changing an SOP

Choose **Edit SOP**. That starts a draft from the current version, and members keep seeing the current one until you publish. Say what changed. The previous version is kept and marked as replaced, and only the current version can be opened, so nobody follows an old procedure by mistake.

## Keeping them current

Under **Settings**, set the owner, category, and how often it needs reviewing. When a review comes up, the owner is reminded.

If you've checked the current version and it's still right, choose to confirm the review. That doesn't need a new version.

## Archiving

Choose **Archive** at the top of an SOP's page when it's no longer in use. Members stop seeing it, and lessons stop listing it. Its versions and files are kept.

Archived SOPs are hidden from the library until you set the status filter to **Archived**. Open one there, and **Delete** at the top removes it for good, with all its versions and files. It can't be undone, though the audit log keeps a note of what was deleted. If a live or draft course still lists the SOP, remove it from that lesson first.

## Attaching to lessons

Attach SOPs to a lesson under [Curriculum](admin-curriculum.md). The lesson always shows the current version, so you only update it in one place.

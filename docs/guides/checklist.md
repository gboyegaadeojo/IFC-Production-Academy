# Checklist

**Checklist** is what each position does before and after a service. It opens to today's service, or the next one.

When Messages is switched on, phones show the checklist under **Serve** on the bottom bar. The **SOPs** button at the top of the checklist opens the SOPs.

## Using it

When you're scheduled, the **Checklist** button on your serving card on Training, and **Open checklist** on Schedule, take you to that service with your position's checklist open. If you serve in two positions, each one opens its own.

Your own department's checklist comes first, marked **YOU**. On a computer, choose another department from the list on the left to see theirs. On a phone or iPad, the button at the top names the checklist you're looking at: tap it, and a sheet slides up listing every checklist with how much is done. Tap one to open it, or drag the sheet down to close it. Tick items as you go. The tick shows straight away and saves in the background. If a save fails, the tick goes back and tells you why.

Everyone sees each other's ticks within about 30 seconds, without reloading.

## When you can tick

Ticking opens two days before the service and closes twelve hours after it. Outside that, you can read it but not tick.

## Who can see it

The Checklist is for the media team. Your access comes from your team in Planning Center. If you can't open it and think you should, ask a scheduling administrator to add you to a team.

## If you're leading the service

Positions that lead the service, such as Technical Director, can also:

- **Reset checklists:** clears every tick for this service, for when it was ticked by mistake. The ticks are kept in the record.
- **Undo the last reset:** brings those ticks back. Anything ticked again since stays as it is.

## On the Service Dashboard

The [Service Dashboard](service-dashboard.md) has a Checklist tile. A tick there is the same tick as here, with the same rules. The wall TV only shows totals.

## After the service

Each service keeps its own record of what was done and what was missed. Every service starts fresh. Leaders can see past records under [Admin](admin-checklists.md).

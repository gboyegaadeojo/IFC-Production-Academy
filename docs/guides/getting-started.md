# Getting started

The Academy is where the production team at Immanuel Fellowship Church learns, gets ready, and sees what's happening on a Sunday. This page gets you signed in and shows you around.

## Signing in

- **If you're on a production team in Planning Center,** choose the Planning Center button. You don't need a separate password.
- **If you're not,** choose **Sign in with an email link**, type your email, and open the link we send you. It works once. There's no password to remember.
- **If you'd like to join the team,** choose **Join the production team** on the sign-in page and fill in the form. You don't need an account for it.

## Your profile

Open your name, then choose your name at the top of the menu, to see the quizzes you've passed, how many lessons you've completed, and your best score on each quiz. Open a quiz to see each try. Only you and your leaders can see these. They don't decide whether you serve.

## Finding your way around

The side bar on the left has these places, each an icon with its name. On a phone, five of them are along the bottom, and **Explore** is at the top of **Courses**.

| Place | What it's for | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Training** | Departments, courses, and how far you've got | [Training](training.md) |
| **Courses, Explore** | The Library: find any course or video, with search and filters | [Training](training.md) |
| **Schedule** | When you're serving, and who with | [Schedule](schedule.md) |
| **Checklist** | What to do before and after this service | [Checklist](checklist.md) |
| **SOPs** | The team's written procedures | [SOPs](sops.md) |

If you're on a production team and using a computer, you also get the [Service Dashboard](service-dashboard.md).

Beside your name are the sun or moon (light or dark) and the bell (notifications). On a phone they're at the top right; on a tablet or computer they're at the bottom of the side bar. Your name opens a menu with the rest: choose your name at the top of it for your profile, then mentoring, getting the app, help, and signing out. Leaders also see **Admin** there.

## Three things that are kept separate

People often assume these are one thing. They aren't.

1. **Finishing a course** means you did the lessons and passed the quiz.
2. **A competency** means someone watched you do the job and you met the checklist.
3. **Approved to Serve** follows by itself once you hold every competency a role needs.

Watching videos alone never approves you to serve. And being approved doesn't put you on the schedule: team managers decide who serves, and they can add you while you're still learning.

## Your profile

Open your name, then choose your name at the top of the menu. Your profile shows the name leaders see, with **Edit name** under it, then three totals (lessons done, quizzes passed, and roles you're approved in), **Approved to serve** for each role, your departments and how far you've got, your quizzes, your certificates, and how you sign in. If you signed in with Planning Center and your name differs there, you can use that one.

## Notifications

Until the Academy launches, notifications stay in the app. Choose the bell beside your name. A dot on it means something is unread. The bell opens a panel with your newest notifications, such as being scheduled or an assessment result. On a phone it slides up from the bottom. Choose the bell again, or anywhere outside the panel, to close it.

- **Mark all read** clears the dot. It's greyed out when everything is already read.
- **See all notifications** opens the full list.
- The settings button (the sliders) opens **Notification settings**.

### Notification settings

Here you turn on alerts for the device you're using, and choose what also reaches your phone or your email. There's one choice for each group: **Serving**, **Training**, and, for leaders, **Leading**. Each has a **Phone** button and an **Email** button. A tick means it's on. A dash means only some kinds in that group are on.

To choose kinds one by one, open **Fine-tune each alert**. Most people never need it.

New courses, certificates, and SOP updates stay in the app. Once your phone has alerts on, you only get an email if a service is moved or cancelled, or for anything you've turned off for your phone. Every email has a link back to these settings.

## Put it on your phone

Open your name, then **Get the app**. It's free, takes a minute, and doesn't use an app store.

- **iPhone or iPad:** open the Academy in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
- **Android:** open the Academy in Chrome and tap **Install app**.
- **Computer:** in Chrome or Edge, select the install icon at the right of the address bar.

### Alerts

On the same page, choose **Turn on alerts** to get updates that drop down like any other app. If you're on a team, the app asks you to do this on your phone each time you open it, until it's done. **Not now** puts it off until next time, and nothing is locked in the meantime. If your phone can't do alerts, choose **My phone can't do this**. Your leaders will see that, and you'll get emails instead. You turn alerts on separately for each device. Alerts wait until morning between 8 pm and 8 am. On iPhone and iPad, alerts need iOS 16.4 or later, and they only work once the Academy is on your Home Screen.

## Dark or light

Tap the sun or moon beside your name to change between light and dark: one tap switches. It's remembered on each device.

## Want to join the team?

If you're not serving yet, the **Serve with us** page is the place to start. Tell us your name and what interests you, and a leader will be in touch within a few days. No experience is needed.

## Getting more help

Open your name, then **Help**, from any page. If you're on a screen with its own guide, that's where it opens. For anything else, ask your department leader or the Production Director.

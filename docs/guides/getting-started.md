# Getting started

The Academy is where the production team at Immanuel Fellowship Church learns, gets ready, and sees what's happening on a Sunday. This page gets you signed in and shows you around.

## Signing in

- **If you're on a production team in Planning Center,** choose the Planning Center button. You don't need a separate password.
- **If you're not,** choose **Sign in with an email link**, type your email, and open the link we send you. It works once. There's no password to remember.
- **If you'd like to join the team,** choose **Join the production team** on the sign-in page and fill in the form. You don't need an account for it.

You can read these guides without signing in: choose **Help and guides** on the sign-in page. Choose **Sign in** at the top to go back.

## Your profile

Open your name, then choose your name at the top of the menu, to see the quizzes you've passed, how many lessons you've completed, and your best score on each quiz. Open a quiz to see each try. Only you and your leaders can see these. They don't decide whether you serve.

## Finding your way around

On a tablet or computer, the side bar on the left lists these places, each an icon with its name, under **Learn** and **Serve**. The small round arrow on the side bar's right edge, which shows when you point at the side bar, folds it down to icons only (hover over an icon to see its name), and the device remembers your choice. On a phone, five of them are on the bar floating along the bottom: a soft purple highlight slides to the one you're on. **Explore** is at the top of **Courses** there.

| **Training** | Departments, courses, and how far you've got | [Training](training.md) |
| **Courses, Explore** | The Library: find any course or video, with search and filters | [Training](training.md) |

When Messages is switched on, it sits under **Training** in the side bar, with how many are unread, and in the middle of the bar along the bottom of a phone. On a phone, **Checklist** and **SOPs** then share one tab, **Serve**, which opens the checklist and has an **SOPs** button at the top.

If you're on a production team and using a computer, you also get the [Service Dashboard](service-dashboard.md).

Beside your name is the bell (notifications). On a phone your name, the bell, and the sun or moon (light or dark) are at the top right. On a tablet or computer your name and the bell are at the bottom of the side bar, and light or dark is in the menu your name opens. Your name opens a menu with the rest: choose your name at the top of it for your profile, then mentoring, getting the app, help, and signing out. Leaders also see **Admin** there.

## Three things that are kept separate

People often assume these are one thing. They aren't.

1. **Finishing a course** means you did the lessons and passed the quiz.
2. **A competency** means someone watched you do the job and you met the checklist.
3. **Approved to Serve** follows by itself once you hold every competency a role needs.

Watching videos alone never approves you to serve. And being approved doesn't put you on the schedule: team managers decide who serves, and they can add you while you're still learning.

## Your profile

Open your name, then choose your name at the top of the menu. Your profile shows the name leaders see, with **Edit name** under it, then three totals (lessons done, quizzes passed, and roles you're approved in), **Approved to serve** for each role, your departments and how far you've got, your quizzes, your certificates, and how you sign in. If you signed in with Planning Center and your name differs there, you can use that one.

## Notifications

Notifications come to your phone and email as you've chosen, and they're all in the app too. Choose the bell beside your name. On a phone a dot on it means something is unread; in the side bar it shows how many. The bell opens a panel with your newest notifications, such as being scheduled or an assessment result. On a phone it pulls down from under the top bar, with a point at the bell. Choose the bell again, anywhere outside the panel, or the handle at its foot to close it.

New notifications are listed first, under **New**, and the rest under **Earlier**. When there's more than one kind, choose **All**, **Serving**, **Training** or **Leading** at the top to see just those.

Each notification has an icon for what it's about (serving, training, or leading) and a line in purple saying what tapping it does, such as **Confirm or decline**.

You're also told when a leader changes something for you: a role given or removed, a request to join turned down, being removed from a department, a skill paused, a certificate withdrawn, another try at a quiz, being taken off a service, mentoring ending, or a course no longer assigned.

- **Mark all read** clears the dot and keeps your notifications. It's greyed out when everything is already read.
- **Clear all** takes every notification out of the list, after asking you first. To clear just one, choose the **×** beside it. Cleared notifications don't come back, and new ones still arrive as usual.
- **See all notifications** opens the full list.
- The settings button (the sliders) opens **Notification settings**.

### Notification settings

Here you turn on alerts for the device you're using, and choose what also reaches your phone or your email. There's one choice for each group: **Serving**, **Training**, for leaders **Leading**, and, when Messages is switched on, **Messages**. Each has a **Phone** button and an **Email** button, except Messages, which is never emailed and has only **Phone**. A tick means it's on. A dash means only some kinds in that group are on.

To choose kinds one by one, open **Fine-tune each alert**. Most people never need it.

New courses, certificates, and SOP updates stay in the app. Once your phone has alerts on, you only get an email if a service is moved or cancelled, or for anything you've turned off for your phone. Every email has a link back to these settings.

## Put it on your phone

Open your name, then **Get the app**. It's free, takes a minute, and doesn't use an app store.

- **iPhone or iPad:** open the Academy in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
- **Android:** open the Academy in Chrome and tap **Install app**.
- **Computer:** in Chrome or Edge, select the install icon at the right of the address bar.

The app keeps itself up to date. When a new version is out, it loads it the next time you come back to the app, or when you move to another page if you're in the middle of typing. You never need to delete it and add it again.

### Alerts

On the same page, choose **Turn on alerts** to get updates that drop down like any other app. If you're on a team, the app asks you to do this on your phone each time you open it, until it's done. **Not now** puts it off until next time, and nothing is locked in the meantime. If your phone can't do alerts, choose **My phone can't do this**. Your leaders will see that, and you'll get emails instead. You turn alerts on separately for each device, and they come at any time of day (your phone's Do Not Disturb still applies). On iPhone and iPad, alerts need iOS 16.4 or later, and they only work once the Academy is on your Home Screen.

### The number on the app's icon

Once the Academy is on your Home Screen, its icon shows how many notifications and messages you haven't read yet, added together, like other apps. It goes down as you read them, and it updates when an alert arrives, even with the app closed. On iPhone and iPad it needs iOS 16.4 or later and alerts turned on. On an installed computer app it shows on the taskbar or dock. Android shows its own dot on the icon instead of a number.

## Dark or light

On a phone, tap the sun or moon beside your name to change between light and dark: one tap switches. On a tablet or computer, open your name and use the **Dark mode** switch at the top of the menu. It's remembered on each device.

## Want to join the team?

If you're not serving yet, the **Serve with us** page is the place to start. Tell us your name and what interests you, and a leader will be in touch within a few days. No experience is needed.

## Getting more help

Open your name, then **Help**, from any page. If you're on a screen with its own guide, that's where it opens. For anything else, ask your department leader or the Production Director.

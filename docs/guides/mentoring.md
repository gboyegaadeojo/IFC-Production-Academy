# Mentoring

A department leader can pair you with a mentor: a trainer or leader who helps you grow. To find it, open your name, then **Mentoring**. If you'd like a mentor and don't have one, ask your department leader.

## What's in a pairing

| Part | Who can read it |
| --- | --- |
| **Goals** | Both of you and the department's leaders |
| **Shared notes** | Both of you and the department's leaders |
| **Your private notes** | Only you. They never appear in reports or exports |

Add a goal with an optional follow-up date. Add shared notes for anything you'd like to keep together, such as feedback after a service.

When a pairing ends, it stays here, marked as ended, for reference. You both get a notification when it ends.

Leaders set up pairings under [Admin, Mentoring](admin-reports-mentoring-audit.md).

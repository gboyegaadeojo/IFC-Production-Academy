# Messages

**Messages** is where the production team talks: a chat for each department, group chats, and direct messages between two people. It's there once a Platform Administrator switches it on under **Admin → Features**.

## Finding it

- **On a computer or tablet:** choose **Messages** in the side bar, after **Schedule**.
- **On a phone:** choose **Messages** in the middle of the bottom bar.

A number on Messages shows how many messages you haven't read. It updates about once a minute, and whenever you move to another page.

## Your chats

The list shows your chats in three groups, each with its last message, the time, and how many are unread:

- **Group chats**, such as one for everyone in the Academy.
- **Department chats:** one for each department. You're in a department's chat if you're a member of the department, on one of its serving teams in Planning Center, or one of its leaders. Joining or leaving changes this straight away.
- **Direct messages** between you and one other person.

Type in **Search chats** to find one by name or by what was said last.

## Talking in a chat

Open a chat, write in the box at the bottom, and choose **Send**. On a computer, Enter sends too, and Shift and Enter starts a new line. New messages appear as they're sent, without refreshing, while the chat is open.

Leaders have a **Leader** label beside their name.

To answer a particular message, choose the **…** beside it, then **Reply**. Your message shows what it's answering.

If you lose your connection, the chat says **You're offline**. What you write waits, and sends when you're back. If a message says **Not sent**, choose **Try again**.

## Photos, GIFs and documents

Choose the **+** beside the message box:

- **Photo:** choose up to 4. They're made smaller on your phone before they're sent, which also removes where they were taken. The chat shows small versions; tap one to see it full size.
- **GIF:** search GIPHY and tap one to send it. Only GIFs rated G appear. What you type in the search goes to GIPHY.
- **Document:** a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text file, up to 10 MB, in a message of its own. Others in the chat tap it to download it. Programs, and files that aren't what their name says, are refused.

Photos and documents are kept for 90 days, and messages for 3 years, unless a Platform Administrator changes them. After that the message stays, and says the photo or file is no longer available. Save anything you need to keep.

## Starting a direct message

Choose the pencil at the top of the list, find the person, and choose them. You can message anyone in the Academy.

Direct messages are private between the two of you. Nobody else can read them, including leaders, the Production Director and Platform Administrators.

## Group chats

Department Leaders, the Production Director and Platform Administrators can make group chats. Choose the pencil, then **New group chat**:

1. Give the group a name.
2. Leave **Everyone in the Academy** on for a chat with everyone. New people join it automatically, and anyone who leaves the Academy leaves it. Or turn it off and choose people.
3. Choose **Make the group**.

Everyone in a group can write in it. The person who made it can rename it from the **…** at the top right of the chat.

## Deleting a message

Choose the **…** beside a message, then **Delete message**, and confirm. It's removed for everyone in the chat.

- **You** can delete your own messages.
- **A department's leaders** can delete any message in their department's chat. It then shows as **Message deleted by a leader**.
- **In a group chat,** the person who made it, the Production Director and Platform Administrators can delete any message.

Nobody else can delete someone's direct messages. Every deletion goes in the audit log, with who deleted it and when. The log never keeps what the message said.

## Being told about new messages

**On your phone, alerts come straight away** when someone sends a message, if you've turned on alerts for your device. Each chat has one alert that updates as more arrive, for example "4 new messages in Camera Operations", so a busy chat doesn't buzz again and again: it waits at least 20 seconds before alerting you again. A direct message alert says who it's from, but never what they wrote.

You aren't alerted for your own messages, a chat you've muted, or a chat you have open on screen.

**New messages are never emailed.** To hear about them away from the app, turn on alerts on your phone.

Under the bell, then **Settings**, the **Messages** group has a **Phone** button: turn it off to stop message alerts. New messages always show under the bell. Nothing reaches your phone between 20:00 and 08:00.

## Earlier messages and who's in a chat

A chat opens on its latest messages. To read further back, scroll to the top and choose **Show earlier messages**.

To see who's in a chat, choose the **…** at the top right, then **People in this chat**. Leaders have a **Leader** label.

- **Department chats** follow the department: joining it, or being on one of its serving teams, puts you in.
- **Everyone in the Academy** includes everyone, and new people join automatically.
- **Group chats of chosen people:** the person who made the group can add people (find them under **Add someone**) and remove them. Anyone in the group can choose **Leave this group**. Someone who leaves or is removed can't read it any more. Adding, removing and leaving go in the audit log.

## Quieter chats

Choose the **…** at the top right of a chat, then **Mute notifications**, to stop being told about that chat. Muted chats don't add to the number on Messages.

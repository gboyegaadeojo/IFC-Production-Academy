# Schedule

**Schedule** shows when you're serving and who with.

## Reminders

The Academy reminds you if you haven't answered after two days, two days before you serve, and on the morning you serve. Planning Center sends its own reminders too, so you may get both. Between 8 pm and 8 am reminders wait until the morning.

## Planning Center does the scheduling

Leaders schedule in Planning Center, and the Academy reads from it every 15 minutes. So some things you do there, not here:

- **Accept or decline** a request. Choose **Accept or decline in Planning Center**.
- **Block out dates** you're away. Choose **Manage in Planning Center**.

If something you changed there doesn't show yet, wait up to 15 minutes. The page says when it last read from Planning Center.

## What you'll see

- **Today**, if you're serving today.
- **Upcoming assignments:** each service, your position, and the time. Times are always Winnipeg time.
- **Calendar:** your month at a glance, with a key to what each mark means. Tap a day to see it.
- **Blocked dates** coming up.
- **Who's serving with you** on each service. Choose it to see the team.
- **Open checklist** takes you to that service's [Checklist](checklist.md).

## Add it to your own calendar

Choose **Add to your calendar (.ics)** to subscribe from your phone or computer's calendar app, so your assignments show up there too.

## You aren't on the schedule yet?

Team managers add people to position teams in Planning Center when they're ready, and they schedule from those teams. Training shows beside your name as "Trained" or "Still learning", but it doesn't decide this. If you think you should be on a team, talk to your department leader.

## If the Academy schedules instead

An administrator can switch scheduling from Planning Center to the Academy. If your church has done that, the page also offers **Days you're away**, **Ask for cover**, and **Open spots** to sign up for, and you accept or decline right here. If you decline, your scheduler is told, and the Academy asks a teammate who's free that day. It isn't switched on today.

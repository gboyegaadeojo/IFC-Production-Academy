# Schedule

**Schedule** shows when you're serving and who with.

## Reminders

The Academy reminds you if you haven't answered after two days, two days before you serve, and on the morning you serve. Planning Center sends its own reminders too, so you may get both. Between 8 pm and 8 am reminders wait until the morning.

## Planning Center does the scheduling

Leaders schedule in Planning Center, and the Academy reads from it every 15 minutes. So some things you do there, not here:

- **Accept or decline** a request. Choose **Accept or decline in Planning Center**.
- **Block out dates** you're away. Choose **Manage in Planning Center**.

If something you changed there doesn't show yet, wait up to 15 minutes. The page says when it last read from Planning Center.

## What you'll see

- **Today**, if you're serving today.
- **Upcoming assignments:** each service, your position, and the time. Times are always Winnipeg time.
- **Calendar:** your month at a glance, with a key to what each mark means. Tap a day to see it.
- **Blocked dates** coming up.
- **Who's serving with you** on each service. Choose it to see the team.
- **Open checklist** takes you to that service's [Checklist](checklist.md).

## Add it to your own calendar

Choose **Add to your calendar (.ics)** to subscribe from your phone or computer's calendar app, so your assignments show up there too.

## You aren't on the schedule yet?

Team managers add people to position teams in Planning Center when they're ready, and they schedule from those teams. Training shows beside your name as "Trained" or "Still learning", but it doesn't decide this. If you think you should be on a team, talk to your department leader.

## If the Academy schedules instead

An administrator can switch scheduling from Planning Center to the Academy. It isn't switched on today. The page looks the same, with the calendar, the day you've chosen, upcoming assignments and blocked dates, and under the title it says **Scheduled in the Academy**. What changes:

- **Answer here.** On the day, choose **Confirm** or **Decline**. If you decline, your scheduler is told, and the Academy asks a teammate who's free that day.
- **Can't make it?** Choose **Ask for cover** on the day, with a note if you like. Teammates who are free are asked, and you stay scheduled until someone takes it.
- **Can you cover?** and **Open spots:** cover a teammate who can't make it, or sign up for a place that still needs someone on a day you're free.
- **Blockouts:** add days you can't serve (one day, a date range, part of a day, or repeating, such as every 1st Sunday, each with an optional reason your scheduler sees), and remove them. Here too are your **Calendar link**, which you add to Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar once so your serving stays up to date there (making a new link stops the old one), and **How I like to serve** (at most so many times a month, and whether to serve with someone in your household, or never at the same service).
- **Files for your services:** stage plots and run sheets for services you've been asked to.

**On a computer**, **Can you cover?** and **Open spots** are cards beside the calendar, and **Calendar link**, **How I like to serve** and **Files** open a panel on the right. **Manage blocked dates** opens the same panel.

**On a phone**, three tabs sit under the title: **My schedule** (this page), **Open spots** and **Blockouts**. The other two open as a sheet from the bottom. In **Open spots**, each row is tagged **Cover** or **Open spot**, so you can tell them apart. Close a sheet by dragging its handle down, tapping outside it, or with **×**. On Android the back button closes it too.

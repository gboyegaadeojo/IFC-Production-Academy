# SOPs

SOPs are the team's written procedures: how to start the stream, how to set up a camera, what to do when something goes wrong.

## Finding one

Open **SOPs** in the side bar and start typing to search. On a phone with Messages switched on, choose **Serve** on the bottom bar, then **SOPs** at the top of the checklist. Results update as you type, and search looks in titles, summaries, and categories. You can also choose a category, see what was **Recently updated**, or narrow it by department. Some SOPs are for **All of production** rather than one department.

The list shows when each SOP was last reviewed, and marks any that are due for review. Open an SOP to see its summary and owner.

## Reading one

Open an SOP to read its steps, and open its file if it has one, usually a PDF. Some have both, such as written steps and a wiring diagram.

- **Only the current version can be opened,** so nobody follows an old procedure by mistake. Version history shows what changed and when.
- **"Due for review"** means nobody has confirmed it's still right lately. It's probably fine, but check with its owner if something looks off.
- **"Archived"** means it's no longer in use. It's kept for the record.

## Where else you'll see them

Lessons can attach SOPs, and always show the current version. The [Service Dashboard](service-dashboard.md) can also have shortcuts to them.

Leaders look after these under [Admin, SOP library](admin-sops.md).

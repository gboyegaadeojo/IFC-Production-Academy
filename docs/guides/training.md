# Training

**Training** is your home in the Academy. From the top:

- **A greeting**, then, if you're on the schedule, a bold card for your next service: when, your position, and buttons for **Open checklist** and **Schedule**. If you haven't replied yet, it asks you to **Reply on Schedule**, with the **Checklist** beside it.
- **Pick up where you left off:** the one thing to do next, with a button to carry on.
- **Coming up:** anything else, such as courses a leader assigned, other services this week, practicals, and other courses you've started.
- **Shortcuts** on a phone: Checklist, Schedule, SOPs, and Mentoring.
- **Your departments**, in a row you can scroll sideways. Each shows how many of its lessons you've marked complete.
- **Recently watched:** the lessons you opened lately. It shows roughly how much of a video you watched, as a reminder only, and a tick once you've marked a lesson **Completed**.
- **Keep exploring:** a few courses to try, in a row you can scroll sideways.

In a row that scrolls sideways, the next card peeks in at the edge, and the dots underneath show where you are. On a tablet or computer, use the arrow buttons beside the heading.

On a wide screen, **This month** (your services) sits beside your next service. Choose one to do what's next: if it says **Please reply**, it opens Schedule; otherwise it opens that service's checklist on your position. Your notifications are under the bell.

**Getting started** shows instead of the plan when you're new: add your name, start your first course, join a department, and get the app.

 The side bar has **Courses** and **Explore**. On a phone, choose **Courses** on the bottom bar, then switch between **Courses** and **Explore** at the top.

## Anyone can learn anything

Every open department's courses are open to you. You don't need anyone's permission to start one, and you can take a course simply because you're curious.

**Joining a department is a separate thing.** It means you're part of that department's team, so its leader knows you and can plan around you. To join, choose **Request to join** on a department. A leader reviews each request, and some departments are by invitation only.

## The Library

- **Courses** lists every course, with search and filters for category, level, and topic. At the top, **Browse by department** is a row you can scroll sideways: tap a department to see only its courses, and tap it again to see them all. On a phone, courses come in rows you scroll sideways: **Continue** for the ones you've started, then a row for each category. **See all** shows one category's courses on their own. Searching or choosing a filter shows the matching courses together.
- **Explore** finds courses and single videos, whichever department they're in. It opens on its search and filters for level, category, and topic. Once you search or choose one, matching courses come first, with single videos below them. Opening a video takes you to that lesson in its course, and it counts there like any other lesson.

## General categories

**General categories**, such as General and Leadership, are courses for everyone on the production team, whichever department you serve in. There's nothing to join: open a topic and start a course. Finishing one earns a certificate, but it doesn't count towards being Approved to Serve.

## Assigned to you

If a leader asks you to take a course, it shows on Training until you finish it, marked **Assigned by a leader**. Go at your own pace. It doesn't stop you doing anything else, and it doesn't put you on a team.

A leader can give it a due date. The notification says when, and you're told if the date changes or is taken off. A due date is a date to aim for: missing it never stops you doing anything.

## Start here

Some courses, such as A Guide to Serving, are marked **Start here**. The ones you haven't finished show in your plan on Training, and they're listed under **Before you start** on each department's page. They're a good place to begin, but nothing is locked until you've done them.

Choosing a course anywhere opens its summary, with a button to start or continue. To jump straight back in, use **Continue** under **Pick up where you left off** on Training.

## How a department is set out

Each department has three levels, one step at a time. Each level has courses, and each course has lessons, and usually a quiz and a practical assessment.

A department's page opens with its colour, name and what it's for, with its course covers fanned out beside them. Beside or below that, **Up next** shows the cover of the course to carry on with, how much of the department you've done, and a **Continue** button. On a phone, once you scroll past it, a slim **Up next** bar above the tabs keeps Continue in reach.

**Your path** shows the levels in order, each with a ring of the lessons you've done. The level you're working on opens first. Choose any level to open or close it and see its courses, each with its own cover.

**What each role still needs** lists each serving role in the department as a short checklist: being a member, then each practical skill the role needs, ticked once you've passed it. Courses aren't on it, because finishing them never approves you to serve on its own.

On Training, each department you belong to has a bar showing how many of its lessons you've marked complete, with the count beside it, such as **3 of 10 lessons**. When a new level opens, the bar drops because there are more lessons to do, and what you've finished still counts. A full bar means the lessons so far are complete. It doesn't mean you're approved to serve.

| Label on a department | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **See courses** | It's open. Go ahead. |
| **Coming soon** | You can see what's planned, but training opens when the curriculum is ready. |
| **Join** | Anyone can join this department straight away. |

## Taking a course

Open a course to see its cover, how long it takes, and what it covers. Choose **Start the course**, or **Continue** to pick up where you left off.

On the course page, **About** says what you'll learn and **Quiz results** lists the quizzes you've taken. The **Lessons** panel shows your progress and every lesson in order, with each quiz and practical in its place. On a phone, the lessons come first.

### Starting over

Open a course you've started, choose **Actions** at the top right, then **Start over** to begin it again from the first lesson. The app warns you first and asks you to confirm.

It clears your completed lessons, your place in each video, and your quiz results for that course, so you'll retake its quizzes. Your certificate, any practical you've passed, and whether you're approved to serve all stay as they are. Leaders' reports show your new progress. You can't undo it yourself, but your old results are kept on record.

### Lessons

A lesson may have a video, what you'll learn, and attached SOPs. When you've done it, mark it **Completed** yourself. The Academy never claims a video was fully watched, so it's on your honour.

Some lessons are optional. The course outline marks them. Only the ones needed **To finish the course** count.

### Quizzes

The app scores your answers against the pass mark the department set. That's usually 80%, with 3 attempts, though a department can change either.

- If you don't pass, go back over the lessons and try again.
- If you've used all your attempts, talk to your trainer or leader. They can give you another one.
- You can leave and come back: an unfinished attempt waits for you under **Resume the quiz**.

Passing a quiz counts towards the course. It doesn't approve you to serve.

### Practical assessments

This is where someone watches you do the job.

1. Open the practical, and read **What the trainer watches for**.
2. When you're ready, choose **Request assessment**. A trainer will arrange a time with you.
3. They tick what they saw on the checklist. You don't get a score from them: the app works out the result from the department's pass rule.

The result is **Pass** or **Not yet**. "Not yet" has no penalty and no waiting period. Practise, and ask again when you're ready.

A pass grants the competency. If that was the last one a role needs, you're **Approved to Serve** in that role automatically.

## Certificates

When you finish a course, its certificate appears on the course and on your profile, ready to download. Each one has a reference and a QR code, so anyone can check it's real. If a certificate was issued by mistake, a leader can withdraw it. It then says so, and the record is kept. You get a notification if one of yours is withdrawn.

## Your next steps

If you're being welcomed onto the team through an onboarding pathway, **Your next steps** shows at the top of Training: where you are, who your contact is, and what's next. Some steps move on by themselves when you finish a course.

Finishing courses never decides on its own whether you serve. A team leader adds you to a team when you're ready.

## Lesson content changes

Leaders update courses over time. What you've already earned stays linked to the version you earned it on, so an update never takes away your progress or certificate.

# Video and graphics approvals

Everything shown during a livestream is checked for copyright first. This replaces the old JotForm
"Video / Graphics Approval Form". It's switched on by an admin once Quality control and the emails
are set up; until then the form says requests are paused.

## Asking to show a video or graphic

Anyone can ask, with no account, at **/media-request** (for example
`https://your-academy-address/media-request`). Share that link where the JotForm link used to be.

1. Enter your name and email. The answer comes to that email.
2. Choose **A video** or **A graphic**.
   - A video is always a link: YouTube, Vimeo or Google Drive. Make sure anyone with the link can
     view it.
   - A graphic can be uploaded (PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF, up to 20 MB) or linked.
3. Choose the service from the Planning Center schedule, or **Another date…** for an event that
   isn't on it.
4. Say what it's for, such as "a missions update before the offering", and press **Send request**.

**The deadline.** Requests need to arrive a set number of days before the service: 5 days unless an
admin changes it, counted as calendar days or business days (Monday to Friday). If you're cutting it
close, the form tells you. A late request is still sent, marked Late, and may not be checked in
time. An admin can choose to refuse late requests instead.

You can send up to 5 requests a day from one email address.

## Checking on your request

Straight away you get a "We got your request" email with a **See its progress** link. That page
shows:

- **Sent**, with the time.
- **With Quality control**, who look at every request first.
- **Copyright review, if needed.** Only some requests need it.
- **Decision**, approved or not, with any note from the team.

Only people with your email's link can see it. Keep the email: the link is the only way back.

## The answer

You get an email when it's decided: **Approved**, or **Not approved** with what would help. If it
wasn't approved, the email and the progress page have a **Send a new version** button.

## Words we use

| Word            | Meaning                                                                       |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Quality control | The team that checks every request first                                      |
| Copyright review | A closer look at the rights, only when Quality control asks for one          |
| Late            | Sent with less notice than the deadline                                       |
| Reference       | The request's number, such as CR-0142, to quote if you have a question        |

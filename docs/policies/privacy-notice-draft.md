## Your privacy in the Production Academy

Immanuel Fellowship Church (IFC) runs the Production Academy to train and schedule volunteers on
the production team. This notice explains what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep
it, and what you can ask us to do.

### Who is responsible

[G's full name], Production Director, is IFC's privacy officer for the Academy. Contact:
[privacy contact email].

### What we collect, and why

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your name and email address | To sign you in (by email link, with no password) and to send the notifications you choose |
| The departments you join, and your roles | To show you the right training and let leaders manage their teams |
| Your training records: lessons completed, quiz attempts and scores, practical assessments and feedback, certificates, competencies, and approval to serve | To track your progress and keep the team safe and ready. Completing training and being approved to serve are kept separate |
| Schedules and the days you're unavailable | To plan who serves when |
| Mentoring goals and notes | To support your growth. Private notes are seen only by the person who wrote them |

We don't collect your age or birth date, phone numbers, home addresses, or photos of you. We don't sell or share your
information, and we don't use it for advertising.

### Who can see it

- **You** can see your own records.
- **Your department's leaders and trainers** can see your training and serving records for their
  department.
- **The Production Director** can see records across departments.
- **Platform administrators** look after accounts and security. They see what they need for that.
- **Other volunteers** see only your name where it's needed, for example on a schedule.- **Private mentoring notes** are seen only by their author.

Every sensitive action by a leader or administrator is recorded in an audit log.

### Where it's stored

The Academy runs on Supabase (database and sign-in) and Cloudflare (the website), and sends email
through Resend. Encrypted backups are kept in IFC's Google Drive. Some of these services store data
outside Canada [confirm the Supabase region].

### How long we keep it

| Record | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Training, competency, assessment, certificate, and serving records | While your account is active, then 7 years. After that your name and email are removed, and only anonymous counts remain |
| Audit records | 7 years |
| Private mentoring notes | 2 years after the mentoring ends |
| Schedules and availability | 2 years after the event |
| Notifications | 12 months |
| Backups | Up to 12 months |

### Your choices and rights

- **Emails:** choose which notifications you get by email on your Notifications page.
- **See your records:** ask the privacy officer for a copy of what the Academy holds about you.
- **Correct it:** you can change your name on your profile; ask about anything else.
- **Delete your account:** ask the privacy officer. We remove your name and email straight away
  and keep only anonymous records, plus the audit entry that the deletion happened.

We'll answer within 30 days.

### Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date below and tell you in the Academy.

# Testing on real phones

A 15-minute check to do on a real iPhone and a real Android phone before more volunteers join, and
again after a big change. The automated tests cover the screens, but only a real phone shows how
signing in, installing, and alerts feel. Do it with one person who knows the app and one who
doesn't.

Use the live address. Tick each step, and if one doesn't work, use **Report a problem** in your
account menu straight away: it sends the page and phone with your note.

## 1. Sign in (3 minutes)

1. In Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android), open the Academy. You land on **Sign in**.
2. Choose **Sign in with Planning Center** and sign in there. You come back to **Training**, signed in.
3. Close the browser completely, open it again, and go back to the Academy. You're still signed in.

## 2. Find your way around (3 minutes)

1. Use each item on the bottom bar. Every page fits the screen: nothing slides sideways and no text is
   cut off.
2. Open your name (top right). Switch between dark and light. Both are easy to read.
3. Open **Help** and read any guide. Pinch to zoom: the page lets you.

## 3. A service checklist (4 minutes)

1. Open **Serve**. The date tile shows the right day of the week, date, and time, matching Planning
   Center.
2. Tap the checklist button at the top. A sheet slides up with every checklist. Choose another one,
   then your own again.
3. Tick an item. The box ticks straight away. Wait 30 seconds and reload: it's still ticked.
4. Tap **Still to do**. Ticked items hide and the count drops. Tap **All tasks**.
5. Untick the item, so the real checklist is left as it was.

## 4. Install it and get an alert (5 minutes)

1. Open your name, then **Get the app**, and follow the steps for your phone.
   - iPhone: Safari, **Share**, **Add to Home Screen**, **Add**.
   - Android: Chrome, **Install app**.
2. Open the Academy from the Home Screen icon. It opens full screen, without the browser bar, and
   you're still signed in.
3. Choose **Turn on alerts** and allow them. On iPhone this needs iOS 16.4 or later.
4. Ask the other tester to send you a message in **Messages**. An alert drops down within a minute,
   even with the app closed, and the icon shows a number until you read it.

## What to write down

For anything that didn't work: the step number, the phone and its system version (Settings, About),
and what you saw. **Report a problem** fills most of that in for you. Check **Admin → Problem
reports** afterwards to see that both testers' notes arrived.
